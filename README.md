# Vision Financial Calculators

Simple, free tools to help you plan your money and understand how your investments grow.

**Try it here:** [https://microintel.github.io/vision/](https://microintel.github.io/vision/)

## What is this?

Vision is a set of easy-to-use calculators for anyone who wants to plan their savings and investments — no finance background needed. Just enter your numbers using sliders or input boxes, and see your results instantly.

## What can you calculate?

- **SIP Calculator** — See how much your monthly investments (like a SIP) could grow to over time.
- **Lumpsum Calculator** — See how much a one-time investment could grow to.
- **Goal Planning Calculator** — Have a savings goal in mind? Find out how much you need to invest today to reach it.
- **CAGR Calculator** — A complete growth toolkit in one place. Use the dropdown menu to switch between 14 different tools, including:
  - Basic growth rate (CAGR) between two values
  - Future value of your money
  - Present value of a future goal
  - How long it will take to reach a target
  - Year-by-year growth breakdown
  - Growth rate adjusted for inflation
  - Comparing two investments side by side
  - Stock, mutual fund, and business growth rates

## Why you'll like it

- **Instant results** — just move a slider or type a number, and your answer updates right away.
- **Easy to understand** — results are shown with simple charts, tables, and clear numbers (in ₹, with familiar Indian formatting like Lakhs and Crores).
- **Works on any device** — phone, tablet, or computer, the layout adjusts automatically.
- **Light or dark mode** — switch anytime with the toggle button.
- **No sign-up needed** — just open the link and start calculating.

## How to use it

1. Open the link: [https://microintel.github.io/vision/](https://microintel.github.io/vision/)
2. Pick the calculator you need from the home screen.
3. Enter your numbers (investment amount, expected return, time period, etc.).
4. See your results update instantly, with charts and easy explanations.

## A note to keep in mind

These calculators are meant to help you understand and plan — they give estimates based on the numbers you enter, not guaranteed results. Actual investment returns can vary, so it's always a good idea to speak with a financial advisor before making big decisions.
